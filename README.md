# news-image-01

Entry for the make.fun `news-image-01` competition: sum up the world's
news of Friday 13 November 2026 in one image.

The entry is one image, `entry.png`: an editorial illustration of the
day's dominant stories -- oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz under a
darkening sky (Iran's radar strike and the tanker mine blasts), a glowing
microchip (Nvidia's Jensen Huang resigning as CEO), and doves over Europe
(the Ukraine peace talks opening to European participation). The program
(`main.py`) only copies the image to the output directory; the image is
the entry.
